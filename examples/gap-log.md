# Gap log

Every application appends the requirements it exposed here — the skills a
posting asked for that `experience.yaml` marks `conceptual` or `none`.

Read it in two directions:

- **Downwards** it is a record: what each company wanted, and what you could not
  show them.
- **Across** it is a study plan: a requirement that appears five times is worth
  a month of your time; one that appears once is noise.

The script writes entries; you may add notes underneath them.
